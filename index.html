Privacy Policy Last updated: 21/09/2026 version: 1.0.0 This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share information when you use the Car Service Tracking application ("App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy. 1. Summary The App stores the vehicle and maintenance data you enter on our company cloud servers, so that we can operate the App's features and use that data to improve the App. We do not sell your data to third parties. 2. Information the App Stores The App stores the following information, entered by you, on our company cloud servers, in addition to keeping a local copy on your device for offline use: Vehicle details (name, brand, model year, odometer/distance, unit of measurement) Maintenance items you choose to track (parts, categories, due dates, due distances) Service history (dates, costs, notes, actions such as "changed" or "checked") App preferences (default values, alert thresholds, categories, hidden items) Developer/debug logs, if applicable, used for troubleshooting This information is transmitted to and stored on our company servers so we can operate the App's features and use it to improve the App's features, performance, and reliability, as described further in this Policy. 3. Information We Do Not Collect We do not collect, and the App does not transmit to us: Your name, email address, or account credentials (the App does not require an account) Your precise location Your contacts, photos, or messages Payment or financial information (cost fields you enter are for your own tracking only and are not processed as payments) 4. How We Use Your Information We use the information stored on our servers to: Operate core App features (tracking, reminders, notifications, history, and any cross-device or sharing features). Analyze usage patterns and troubleshoot problems, in order to improve the App's features, performance, and reliability. Maintain and secure our servers and services. 5. Sharing With Other Users Certain data may be shared with other users of the App where doing so supports the App's features.
